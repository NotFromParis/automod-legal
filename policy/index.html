Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

In short: Automod never sells data. It processes messages to moderate the servers that added it, keeps each server's sanction history and settings, and only sends message content to an AI if the server enabled the AI mode. You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time.

1. Controller

Automod's developer, NotFromParis. Contact: notfromparis.pro@gmail.com.

2. Data we process

  • Servers: server ID and name, Automod settings (enabled modules, sensitivities, chosen channels and roles, command permissions, welcome/goodbye messages, role menus), IDs of the owner, of the member who added the bot and of bot administrators.
  • Automod support server: if a server's owner (or the member who added the bot) is also a member of Automod's official support server, they automatically get a role there based on their servers' plan (User, Pro user or Max user). This role is visible to other members of the support server; it changes with the plan and is removed when Automod leaves their servers.
  • Sanctions: member ID and username, sanction type, reason, duration, date, moderator ID; for automatic sanctions, an excerpt of the message (max 300 characters) and the channel.
  • Moderation tracking: repeat-offence counters, temporary warnings, staff notes, ongoing summons.
  • Server security (if enabled by the server): the server's audit log (who created, deleted or edited a channel or role, banned or kicked someone, added a bot, edited the server) is read in real time; only a per-member counter stays in memory for 15 minutes. The account creation date is checked on join (new accounts). The captcha code stays in memory during verification. Members, roles and channels bypassed by the server (with the optional end date and who added them) are stored in its settings.
  • Anti Alt Accounts (if the server enables it): when a member is banned, kicked by the staff or timed out, Automod keeps a fingerprint of their account to recognise a new account of the same person: ID, names, creation date, fingerprints of the profile picture and banner, profile colour, decoration and tag, hashed fingerprints of their messages (never the text) and the IDs of the people they wrote to. When a member joins, these public profile details, the invite used and their first messages are compared with those fingerprints.
  • Message content: analysed in real time to detect spam, raids, dangerous links, insults, sexual content, personal data and scams. It is not stored, except the excerpt attached to a sanction and the logs the server chose to post in its own channels. Minimal technical data (time and channel of recent messages) stays in memory for a few minutes for spam detection.
  • Posted images (if anti-scam is on): a thumbnail is downloaded from Discord and reduced to a digital fingerprint, compared with a bank of known scam images (fake giveaways, fake casinos…). The image is neither kept nor sent to any outside service. Only the fingerprints of images recognised as scams are kept, with the servers where they were caught, to protect every AI Automod server.
  • AI Automod network (on by default, the server can turn it off in Protection → Server): when Automod itself sees on a server that an account took part in a raid, posted a scam, triggered anti-nuke or is an alt of another account, that fact is shared with the other AI Automod servers. Only pseudonymised IDs (a one-way fingerprint of the account and server IDs), the type of fact and its date are stored: never a message, a reason, the name of a server or of a staff member. Other servers only see « flagged on N other servers, X days ago », never which ones. The fact is used as a staff alert or as one clue among others; it never leads to a sanction on its own, except for an already known raider who joins during a raid in progress on the server. A server that turns the network off no longer shares or receives anything, and its past reports are deleted.
  • Joins and leaves: used for logs, anti-raid (account age) and the server's welcome/goodbye messages.
  • Subscriptions: server ID, plan, subscription status and end date, Stripe customer and subscription IDs, SumUp payment ID (or Discord entitlement), Discord ID of the subscriber and proof of acceptance of the terms (account, date, terms version). Payment details are handled only by Stripe, SumUp or Discord; no Discord data (server name or ID, username) is sent to SumUp.
  • Web dashboard (log in with Discord): with your consent on Discord's page, Automod receives your ID, username, avatar and your server list with your permissions (« identify » and « guilds » scopes), only to show the servers you can manage. Kept for the session (30 days at most, including across bot restarts, on the host's server, with only a hash of the cookie) and deleted on logout or when the session expires. One strictly necessary session cookie (« am_sid »); no analytics or advertising cookies.
  • Help form (Support page of the website, log in with Discord): your name or nickname, your Discord ID and name, the server you pick (and its plan, which sets the request's priority), the subject, the message and any attachments. The request is sent as a Discord direct message to Automod's developer, attachments included (kept by Discord in that conversation), and kept in their Creator page of the dashboard to answer it. If a ticket is opened, the messages exchanged on the website are kept with the request; the ticket closes 24 h after being marked as solved, unless reopened.

3. Artificial intelligence

Only if the server has the Max plan and enabled the AI mode for a module: the analysed message, up to three previous messages from the same author in the channel and the message it replies to are sent to Mistral AI (France, EU), which only returns a verdict. Automod keeps that verdict in memory for at most 10 minutes and uses it for nothing else.

Server AI key (dashboard → Developer): a server can plug in its own key from an AI provider (for example Mistral AI, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google…). The same data as above is then sent to that provider, chosen by the server and bound by its own terms, instead of Mistral AI. The key is encrypted before being stored, never shown again (only its last 4 characters) nor written to logs, and deleted as soon as the server removes it.

4. Purposes and legal basis

Moderating and securing servers and providing the features they enabled (legitimate interest); managing paid subscriptions (performance of a contract); keeping the bot running and secure (legitimate interest).

5. Retention

Settings, sanctions and moderation tracking: as long as Automod is on the server, or until deletion is requested. Anti-spam technical data: a few minutes, in memory only. AI verdicts: at most 10 minutes, in memory only. Anti Alt Accounts fingerprints: 180 days after a ban, 14 days after a kick, up to 24 h after a timeout ends; deleted as soon as the member is unbanned. AI Automod network: 14 days for a scam (often a hacked account, later recovered), 30 days for joining during a raid, 90 days for raid messages, 180 days for anti-nuke and alt accounts. Ended subscriptions: removed about 45 days after they end. Help form requests: the 300 most recent are kept in the Creator page; a request can be deleted on request.

6. Sharing

Data is never sold, rented or used for advertising. It is only shared with providers required to run the service: Discord, the bot's hosting provider (EU), Mistral AI or the AI provider chosen by the server (AI mode), Stripe and SumUp (payments), Cloudflare (secure delivery of the web dashboard). Moderation logs are posted in the server's own channels.

7. Direct messages

Automod only sends direct messages to notify a member of a sanction, to send a welcome message if the server enabled it, to explain to a too-new account why it was kicked on join (if the server enabled that protection), for exceptional announcements from the developer to server owners and administrators, and for updates on your own help request (ticket opened, new reply, ticket solved).

8. Your rights

You can request access to, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its processing, by writing to notfromparis.pro@gmail.com (include your Discord ID and the server). You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

9. Children

Discord is only available from the minimum age set by its terms. Automod collects no other data than described here, whatever the member's age.

10. Security and changes

Data is stored on the bot's hosting and accessible only to the developer. This policy may change; the last update date is shown at the top of this page.
