What AI Automod does beyond a classic automod

There are plenty of automods, starting with the one built into Discord. Most of them filter words and spam. AI Automod does that too, but above all it has five features you won't find in a classic automod.

An AI that understands tone

It drives the whole bot rather than being one more option. In AI mode, each message is read as a whole: category (insult, sexual content, personal data, scam), severity and tone, joke or serious. “you're so dumb 😂” between friends passes, “you don't even have friends, shut up” is removed. Before any sanction, a second AI checks that the message itself is really at fault: when in doubt, nobody is punished. Max plan, or free with your own AI key. How the AI works →

Convocations

When a member causes trouble, a ban or a mute doesn't always settle it: sometimes you need to talk. /convocate @member [reason] creates a private text and voice channel between the staff and the member, each convocation with its own colour. The staff is notified in its channel with the reason and a deadline, images posted in the convocation are pinned automatically to keep the evidence, /addconvoc and /delconvoc add or remove someone, and /closeconvoc closes everything, even if the member left the server. Pro plan. Convocations →

Moscow Eye

A raid is coming, your server got shared where it shouldn't have? One click on the home page of /controls or the dashboard, and for 24 hours at most server protection is doubled: anti-raid and anti-nuke react twice as early, and the minimum account age on join is doubled. Message moderation doesn't change. It stops by itself. Moscow Eye in detail →

Auto slowmode

A channel gets out of hand (stream, announcement, drama)? When it goes over X messages per second, AI Automod turns on its slowmode by itself, either for a duration you choose or until things calm down. It never lowers a slowmode set by the staff and restores the previous setting at the end. Free plan. Set up auto slowmode →

Anti-scam honeypot

Hacked accounts and bots post their fake gifts where everyone can write: “free Nitro”, “MrBeast casino, use code…”, crypto airdrops, “withdraw” and easy money. The anti-scam watches those public channels and computes a score: typical scam phrases, links, @everyone typed as text, screenshots posted by a new or just-joined account. Above the threshold, its messages are deleted and the account is kicked, not banned, since it's often a hacked friend. No image is ever sent to an AI: everything happens on the spot, in real time. Included in the Free plan.
