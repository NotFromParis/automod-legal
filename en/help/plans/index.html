Free, Pro and Max plans

A plan applies to the whole server. Type /plans on Discord to see the plans and subscribe. Only the owner and the buyer can pay.

🆓 Free

Free forever

  • Ban, kick, mute, warn
  • Anti-spam, anti-raid, anti-mentions, anti-links
  • Anti-nuke, anti bot, anti new accounts
  • Critical server protection
  • Logs and web dashboard
  • Welcome, roles, suggestions, absences

⭐ Pro

€2.99 for 30 days

  • Everything in Free
  • Anti-insults and hate speech
  • Anti-sexual content
  • Anti-personal data
  • Anti-scam (fake Nitro, phishing)
  • Captcha on join
  • Smartlog: logs with action buttons
  • Convocations, bulk sanctions

💎 Max

€9.99 for 30 days

  • Everything in Pro
  • AI mode (beta) on content modules
  • Tells a joke from an insult
  • Fewer false positives
  • Personal onboarding

No automatic renewal: you get a reminder before it ends. Card payment on the provider's secure page, active within a minute. Or plug in your own AI key for AI mode without a subscription.

How it works

  1. /plans (or the end of /setup) shows the plans.
  2. Before any payment, the bot shows a summary and the terms, which you accept.
  3. Card payment happens on the provider's secure page: Automod never sees your card.
  4. The plan turns on by itself within a minute, for 30 days. No automatic renewal: you get a reminder before it ends.

You have 14 days to change your mind (prorated refund), as explained in the terms.

AI without a subscription

Already have a key with an AI provider? Plug it in on the dashboard → Developer: AI modules then work without the Pro or Max plan, and your provider bills you. See your own AI key.
