Anti-raid, anti-nuke and captcha for Discord

Automod protects your server from raids, throwaway accounts and staff members who go rogue. Protections are set in /controls → Protection or on the dashboard.

Modules and their plans

Each module has three states: off, on (rule-based) or AI (Max plan or your own key). The higher the sensitivity, the faster the bot reacts.

  • Free: all rule-based automatic moderation (anti-spam, anti-mentions, anti-links, anti-insults and hate speech, anti-sexual content, anti-personal data, anti-scam) with the standard sensitivity, and server protection: anti-raid, anti-nuke, Anti Bot, Anti New Accounts.
  • Pro: adjustable sensitivity for each module, captcha and critical server protection.
  • Max: AI mode on content modules — it understands context (jokes, games, banter) and makes fewer mistakes. If the AI doesn't answer, the classic rules take over. See the AI.

Anti-raid and automatic raid mode

Anti-raid spots waves of identical messages, messages posted across several channels and mass joins: if N members join within S seconds (8 in 10 s by default), raid mode turns on for M minutes (60 by default) and newcomers are kicked. The three numbers are set with anti-raid's Edit button.

Anti-nuke

Three modules watch the server's audit log: Channel regulation, Role regulation and Mass moderation. Whoever creates, deletes or edits too many channels or roles, or bans, kicks or times out too many people within 10 minutes (threshold based on sensitivity) is sanctioned: all their roles removed, kicked or banned, your choice. The owner, the buyer and anything bypassed are never sanctioned. Automod needs the « View Audit Log » permission.

Critical server protection, Anti Bot, Anti New Accounts

  • Critical server protection (Pro plan): a change to the name, icon, banner, description… made by someone who isn't bypassed is reverted.
  • Anti Bot: a bot added by someone who isn't bypassed is kicked.
  • Anti New Accounts: accounts created less than N days ago (7 by default) are kicked.

Captcha (Pro plan)

Newcomers get the « Unverified » role, which only sees the verification channel. They click Verify and copy the code from an image. Without success within the delay (10 minutes by default) or after 3 mistakes, they are kicked.

  1. /controls → Protection → Raids and joins → Captcha.
  2. Pick the verification channel and create the « Unverified » role.
  3. Prepare channels hides every channel from that role (except verification).
  4. Publish panel posts the Verify button.

👁️ Moscow Eye: doubled protection before a raid

Feel a raid coming (threats, your server shared elsewhere, an announced wave)? Turn on the Moscow Eye on the home page of /controls or the web dashboard. For 24 hours at most, server protection is doubled:

  • Anti-raid: half as many channels, accounts or joins are enough to trigger the defence and the automatic raid mode, and accounts twice as old count as recent.
  • Anti-nuke: the limits on creating or deleting channels and roles and on mass moderation are halved.
  • Anti New Accounts: the minimum account age on join is doubled.

Message moderation doesn't change (insults, spam, links…), and only active protections are strengthened. It stops by itself after 24 hours, or when you turn it off. Each activation is noted in the raid log channel.

During a raid

  • Server lockdown: nobody can write in public channels anymore (administrators keep access). A second click restores the channels.
  • Raid mode: newcomers are kicked as soon as they join, until you turn it off.
  • Pause Automod in some channels: messages in those channels are no longer analysed.
  • =stop / =start pause or resume the bot (whole server or one channel).

🎫 Bypass

/bypass or =bypass makes Automod ignore a member, a role or a channel — no automatic moderation or protection, no AI — for a given time (=bypass @Partner 2h) or with no limit (=bypass #memes). No permission is given: manual commands still work. =bypass alone shows the list, =bypass remove @x removes. Owner and buyer only by default.
