Frequently asked questions

Another question? Come to the support server.

Is Automod free?

Yes. The Free plan includes manual moderation, anti-spam, anti-raid, anti-nuke, logs and the web dashboard, with no time limit. Pro (€2.99) and Max (€9.99) add content filters, captcha, Smartlog and AI, for 30 days with no automatic renewal.

How does the AI tell a joke from an insult?

For each message, the AI gives the category (insult, sexual content, personal data, scam), the severity and the tone: joke or serious. Banter between friends with “lol” or 😂 counts one level lower than a real attack, and the sensitivity chosen by the server decides whether to sanction.

How do I install Automod?

Add the bot with the “Add to Discord” button, then type /setup on your server: quick mode creates the log channels, detects staff roles and turns on the recommended protections in one click.

Does Automod speak French?

Yes. On the first /setup you choose English or French for the whole server: messages, panels, logs and dashboard.

What does Automod do with my messages?

It analyses them to moderate the server and keeps the sanction history. A message's text is only sent to an AI if the server turned on AI mode. No data is ever sold.

I was sanctioned on a server, what can I do?

Each server sets its own rules and sanctions: contact that server's staff (the sanction DM includes their appeal link if they have one). The Automod team can't lift a sanction for them.
