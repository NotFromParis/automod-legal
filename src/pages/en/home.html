Every server protects the others

AI Automod is a network. A raider, a scammer or an alt account caught on one server is recognised on all the others, and no server ever sees what happens on another.

  • A raid caught here: its accounts are flagged everywhere else.
  • A scam image caught on 2 servers: blocked on all of them.
  • Pseudonymised IDs, never a message or a server name.

What a classic automod doesn't do

There are plenty of automods, starting with Discord's. Here is what AI Automod has on top.

    Convocations

    A private text and voice channel between the staff and the member, evidence pinned, deadline, closed in one command.
