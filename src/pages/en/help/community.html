In /controls → Community (or on the dashboard), each feature has its own page with an ON / OFF button and its settings.

Welcome, goodbye and joins

Welcome and goodbye messages (in a channel or by DM), join and leave feed, auto-roles, member counter, suggestions channel and role menus. Variables you can use in messages:

{user.ping} {user.name} {user.tag} {user.id} {user.created} {user.avatar} {server.name} {server.members} {member.number}

Example: Welcome {user.ping} to {server.name}! You are member #{member.number}.

Reaction roles

Pick roles, give each one an emoji, pick a channel, then Publish: Automod posts the message and adds the reactions. A member who reacts gets the role; removing the reaction removes the role. Up to 20 roles per message, standard or server emojis. Automod's role must be above the roles it gives.

Staff absences

  1. On the Staff absences page, pick the absence role (or Create the « On leave » role) and the absences channel, then Publish panel.
  2. A staff member clicks Report an absence and fills in: from (optional, today by default), until (25/12, 25/12/2026 or a duration such as 3d, 2 weeks) and a reason.
  3. Automod gives them the absence role (on the day if the absence starts later) and announces it in the channel.
  4. On the return date, the role is removed automatically. I'm back ends the absence early; an admin can also end it from /controls.

Only staff can report an absence. A date like 25/12 means “away on the 25th included”.
