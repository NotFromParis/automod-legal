How it works

  1. Type /parrainage on your server (or open the dashboard) to get your link. It is also shown at the end of /setup.
  2. Share it: with a friend who runs a server, in a community, on your socials.
  3. The server adds AI Automod with your link, then takes Pro or Max with /plans.
  4. 14 days after its payment (the refund period), you get your week of Max and they get their month of Pro. You are both told in the Automod channel of your server.

What counts

  • Only a new server counts: a server that already had Automod (even removed and added back) can't be referred.
  • It takes a real payment, not refunded or disputed. A free trial or a personal AI key don't count.
  • Your own server, a server with the same owner, or a server added or paid by your staff don't count.
  • Payment refunded or disputed later: the week and the free month are taken back.
  • The referred server's free month is always Pro, even if it paid for Max.
