By default, Automod follows your server's Discord permissions. You can adjust everything, command by command.

The default

  • Native mode: a command is open to members with the matching Discord permission (/ban → Ban Members).
  • Exception: Automod management commands (/setup, /controls, /bypass, /dashboard, =stop…) are reserved to the owner, the buyer, administrators and bot admins.

Change a permission

In /controls → 🔐 Permissions (or on the dashboard), change each command, or a whole category at once:

  • Discord: the linked Discord permission;
  • Owner: owner, buyer and bot admins only;
  • Roles and members: specific roles and members.

Reset goes back to the default.

Buyer and bot admins

  • The buyer is whoever added Automod if it isn't the owner: they get the same Automod rights as the owner. The owner can remove them at any time.
  • Bot admins (roles or members chosen in /setup) have full access to Automod.

See your commands

/cmds shows each member the commands they can use, /help explains them all with examples.
