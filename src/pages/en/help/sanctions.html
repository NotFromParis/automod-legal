Every command works as a slash command (/ban) and with the = prefix (=ban). Durations: 10m, 2h, 3d, 1w.

Commands

ActionExample
Ban (permanent or temporary)=ban @member 7d toxicity
Kick=kick @member spam
Mute (28 days max)=mute @member 30m flood
Warn=warn @member language
Undo=unban · =unmute · =pardon
A member's history=history @member
Sanction details=case 42 · =editcase · =delcase
Channel tools=purge 20 · =lock · =unlock · =slowmode 30

Cases, logs and appeal link

Every sanction gets a case number, shows up in the logs and the member gets a DM, with the appeal link if you set one in /setup. For automatic sanctions, repeat offences are counted: a member who does it again is sanctioned for longer. =pardon resets their count.

With the Pro plan

  • Sanction several members at once: =ban @a @b 1d raid.
  • Switch any log to Smartlog cards with action buttons (see Logs and Smartlog).

All these sanctions can also be done from the web dashboard (Members and Sanctions tabs).

Convocations (Pro plan)

=convocate @member opens a private text and voice channel between staff and the member to talk about an issue. =closeconvoc closes it. =addconvoc / =delconvoc add or remove someone.
