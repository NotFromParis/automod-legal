Everything /controls does, bigger and more comfortable: the dashboard manages Automod from your browser, on desktop and on mobile.

Open the dashboard

Logging in

Click Log in with Discord. Automod only sees your profile and your server list, never your password. You then see the servers where you can manage Automod. On Discord, /dashboard also gives the link.

  • Opening a server's dashboard requires the /dashboard right (owner, buyer, administrators and bot admins by default).
  • Changing a setting also requires the matching command's right: the bot checks every action again.

Security

  • The site only runs over HTTPS, and the bot rejects any request that doesn't come through it.
  • A saved AI key is encrypted; only its last 4 characters are ever shown.
  • You can log out at any time; the session also expires on its own.
