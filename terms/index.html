5. Automated moderation and artificial intelligence

5.1 What Automod's AI is

AI mode sends the text of the relevant messages to an artificial intelligence model provided by a third party (currently Mistral AI, or the provider a server picks with its own key). The model produces a statistical estimate (category, severity, tone), not a human decision. It can be wrong: misread humour, irony, slang, a language or a context, let a harmful message through (false negative) or flag a harmless one (false positive). The same message may be judged differently from one time to the next, and the model's behaviour may change over time, in particular when the provider updates its models (drift).

5.2 Best-efforts obligation

The developer uses reasonable means to limit errors: cautious rules, a second check before any sanction decided by the AI, and the "when in doubt, nobody is punished" principle. It does not guarantee that every harmful message will be detected, nor that no harmless message will be sanctioned. Automated moderation, with or without AI, is an aid to moderation: it does not replace the staff's own attention.

5.3 Staff stay in control

  • Server staff set each module's sensitivity, can turn AI off, bypass a member, role or channel, and revert any automated sanction (unban, remove a timeout, remove a warning).
  • It is up to them to watch the logs and correct mistakes. A member sanctioned by mistake contacts their server's staff: the developer does not take part in the moderation of servers.
  • Automod is not enough to meet a server's legal obligations. For serious content (threats, child sexual abuse material, danger to someone), staff must act themselves and report it to Discord and, if needed, to the authorities.

5.4 Beta, providers and quotas

  • AI mode is offered as a beta. Models, providers, instructions, thresholds and quotas may change at any time to improve quality, control costs or fix a problem, so the AI's behaviour may change after an update.
  • The AI depends on third-party providers: outages, rate limits, price or terms changes. Each server has a daily quota of AI requests. When the AI is unavailable or the quota is reached, rule-based detection takes over automatically.
  • The developer may limit or suspend the AI on one server or on all of them (abuse, abnormal cost, security incident, provider failure, legal obligation).

5.5 Personal AI key

A server can plug in its own AI key. Requests are then billed by the provider to the key holder, under that provider's terms; the cost shown on the dashboard is an estimate. The developer is not responsible for those charges, nor for a key disclosed by its holder. The key is stored encrypted and may be removed if it is invalid or misused.
